Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Workshop Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to YouTube channels the owner runs. It uses the YouTube API Services.

What the tool accesses

  • Google sign-in token. When the channel owner signs in, Google issues an access token so the tool can upload on the owner's behalf. The token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The owner's own videos and text. The video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and comments the tool uploads are written by the owner and sent only to YouTube.
  • The owner's channel name and ID. Read once after sign-in to confirm the right channel was connected.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share data about any other YouTube user.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke the tool's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings. Revoking it makes the stored token useless. Deleting the token file from the owner's computer removes it completely.
